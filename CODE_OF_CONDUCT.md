# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members of the AI Study Suite community are committed to fostering an open and welcoming environment. We think everyone deserves a project devoid of harassment, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards others

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or following users
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which causes others to feel unsafe, unwelcome, or discriminated against

## Our Responsibilities
Community members who encounter unacceptable behavior should report it to the project maintainer. The project team is obligated to respond to reports of abuse and harassment.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers have the right to remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate these standards.
